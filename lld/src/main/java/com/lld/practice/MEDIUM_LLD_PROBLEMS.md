# Medium LLD Problems

1. **Food Delivery Order Manager** - Design order placement, restaurant assignment, status transitions, and delivery tracking.
2. **Ride Matching Service** - Design rider-driver matching, trip lifecycle, fare computation, and cancellation flow.
3. **Hotel Room Booking** - Design room inventory, date-range availability checks, booking confirmation, and modification.
4. **Digital Wallet** - Design wallet top-up, transfer, transaction history, and balance consistency checks.
5. **Inventory Management System** - Design SKU stock updates, warehouse-level tracking, low-stock alerts, and reservation handling.
6. **Online Auction System** - Design auction creation, bidding rules, winner selection, and settlement flow.
7. **Meeting Room Scheduler** - Design conflict-free booking, recurring meetings, participant invites, and room utilization views.
8. **Coupon and Promotion Engine** - Design coupon validation, eligibility checks, stacking rules, and discount calculation.
9. **Learning Progress Tracker** - Design course progress updates, completion milestones, and learner leaderboard views.
10. **Subscription Billing Manager** - Design plan activation, renewal cycles, prorated upgrades/downgrades, and invoice generation.
